Adjectives · How do you feel about a subject?

Tip! When we say what we like or don't like, we use the word because [bɪˈkɒz] — потому что. Then we add a reason with it is + adjective. Example: I like Maths because it is interesting.
02

Grammar · Like / Don't like

Four patterns to talk about school subjects. Listen and repeat.

Pattern 1 · Positive
I like + subject + because + it is + adjective.
I like Maths because it is interesting.
Pattern 2 · Negative
I don't like + subject + because + it is + adjective.
I don't like Chemistry because it is boring.
Pattern 3 · Favourite
My favourite subject is + subject.
My favourite subject is History.
Pattern 4 · Question
What's your favourite subject? Why do you like it?
— My favourite subject is English because it is fun.
Remember! Don't = do not. We use don't with I, you, we, they. We use doesn't with he, she, it.
Example: She doesn't like Art because it is difficult.

Because always answers the question Why?. Read it like: bi-KOZ.

My Favourite Subject

Hello! My name is Tom. I am ten years old. I go to school in London.

I have got many subjects at school. My favourite subject is Maths because it is interesting. I like numbers and solving problems.

My friend Sam likes History because he likes reading about old times. He thinks it is exciting.

My sister Anna doesn't like Chemistry because it is boring for her. She prefers Literature. She loves reading books and poems.

Our teacher Mrs. Smith teaches English. English is fun! We play games and sing songs in class.

I also like PE because we run and jump. But I don't like Art because I can't draw well. Art is difficult for me.

What about you? What is your favourite subject?
