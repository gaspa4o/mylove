Task 3: Школьный день Тома 📝

Прочитай текст. Выбери правильный глагол в выпадающем списке (am, is, are, have got, has got, can).

Hi! My name Tom. I a 5th-grade student. I a big blue backpack.

Today Monday. My 1st subject Maths. I draw straight lines with my ruler.

The 2nd subject English. We read new books.

The 3rd subject Art. My friend Lucy a beautiful new pencil.

The 4th subject Science. We use a sharpener.

Task 4: Вопросы по тексту ❓

Ответь на вопросы, выбрав правильный вариант.

1. What is Tom's 1st subject?
2. What can Tom do with his ruler?
3. Who has got a beautiful pencil?
4. What is the 4th subject?
