Grammar Time ✍️

Learn how to talk about food you like — or don't!

✅
Positive (+)

I eat

Use it for food you like and eat.

🥕 I eat vegetables.
🍎 I eat fruits.
🐟 I eat fish.
🚫
Negative (–)

I don't eat

Use it for food you don't like or don't eat.

🍔 I don't eat junk food.
🍬 I don't eat sweets.
🍖 I don't eat meat.

🧩 How to build a sentence

I
+
eat
/

+
food
I + eat + cereal.
I + don't eat + sweets.
