Clothes & Weather

Мы носим разную одежду в разную погоду. По-английски это звучит так:

In sunny weather, I wear a T-shirt and shorts.
In rainy weather, I take an umbrella and wear a raincoat.
In snowy weather, I wear a jacket, a scarf and boots.

Полезные фразы

Как спросить о погоде и одежде:

— What's the weather like today?
— It's sunny and warm.
— What do you wear in winter?
— I wear a coat, a hat and gloves.
— Put on your jacket, it's cold!
