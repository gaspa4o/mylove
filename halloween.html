🎭 Глагол "To Be" (am / is / are)

Глагол to be меняется в зависимости от местоимения:

  • I → am
  • He / She / It → is
  • We / You / They → are

👻 I am a ghost.

🧙‍♀️ She is a witch.

🦇 They are bats.

🍬 Much / Many / A lot of

Используем для количества:

  • Much — с неисчисляемыми (water, sugar, blood)
  • Many — с исчисляемыми во мн. ч. (bats, cats, witches)
  • A lot of — с любыми (в утверждениях)

🧪 How much potion?

🦇 How many bats?

🍬 I have a lot of candy!

🔑 Possessive Adjectives (Притяжательные)

Указывают, кому принадлежит предмет. Ставятся перед существительным:

Imy — мой
Youyour — твой
Hehis — его
Sheher — её
Itits — его/её
Weour — наш
Theytheir — их

🎃 This is my pumpkin.

🧹 This is her broom.

🕸️ The spider is in its web.

🏚️ This is our haunted house.

🏠 There is / There are

Используется, чтобы сказать, что где-то что-то есть:

There is (единственное число)

🏠 There is a ghost in the house.

🌙 There is a moon tonight.

There are (множественное число)

🦇 There are many bats in the cave.

🎃 There are three pumpkins.

🎃 👻 🦇

Halloween is a fun holiday. It is on the 31st of October. Children love Halloween very much. They wear funny costumes: witches, ghosts, vampires, black cats, and bats.

People decorate their houses with pumpkins, spiders, and candles. Many children go from house to house. They say "Trick or Treat!" People give them a lot of candy. The witches fly on their brooms in the night.

You can see a big moon and many stars. Bats and owls are everywhere. Some people make Jack-O-Lanterns from pumpkins. Halloween is spooky but very fun!
