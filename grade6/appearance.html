Grammar & Structure

How to describe a person? Use this structure!

1. Height and Body (To Be)

Structure: He / She + is + [adjective]

She is tall. She is slim.
He is short. He is chubby.

2. Face, Hair, Eyes (Have / Has)

Structure: He / She + has + [adjective] + [noun]

She has an oval face.
He has short hair.
She has blue eyes.

3. His / Her (Possessives)

Structure: His / Her + [noun] + is / are + [adjective]

Her hair is long.
His eyes are blue.
Her lips are plump.

Reading Practice

Read the texts and answer the questions below!

Text 1: Emily

This is Emily. She is tall and slim. She has an oval face. She has long, straight dark hair. Her eyes are brown. She has plump lips and a small nose. Emily is very beautiful!
1. Is Emily tall or short?
She is tall.
2. Does she have an oval or square face?
She has an oval face.
3. Is her hair curly or straight?
Her hair is straight.
4. What color are her eyes?
Her eyes are brown.

Text 2: Tom

This is Tom. He is short and chubby. He has a square face. He has short, curly blonde hair. His eyes are blue. He has big ears and a big mouth. Tom is very funny!
1. Is Tom tall or short?
He is short.
2. Does he have an oval or square face?
He has a square face.
3. Is he slim or chubby?
He is chubby.
4. Is his hair straight or curly?
His hair is curly.
